Profile Shield

If someone changes your listing on Google, you will know.

Fama Local keeps a reference copy of your business information and compares it with what is on Google. When it detects a change, it warns you on WhatsApp and in the dashboard, showing how the information was before and how it is now, so you can decide what to do.

✓
Key information monitoredName, main phone, address, opening hours and category.
✓
An alert on WhatsApp and in the dashboardYou see before and after side by side — phone (11) 4002-8922 becoming (11) 3333-1010, for example — and decide in seconds whether the change is legitimate.
✓
Attempts to take ownershipThird-party invitations to take over your listing are declined automatically and recorded for you to review.

For safety, the five main fields are not restored on their own: you decide. The external website address is the exception and is restored automatically. Understand why in the FAQ.

No miracle promises

What Fama Local does, and what it does not promise.

Fama Local does

  • write review replies and publish them with your approval;
  • prepare and publish content according to your settings;
  • warn you when your listing information is changed;
  • show where your business appears in your area, point by point;
  • check whether Google's AI Mode cites you;
  • point out what needs attention first.

Fama Local does not promise

  • to put your business in 1st place;
  • to guarantee a specific number of customers;
  • to control Google's algorithm;
  • to replace the quality of your service, product or care.
The idea is simple: stop your presence on Google from depending only on your memory and on whatever time is left.
